## Editing policy applied in this repo

1. Remove superiority or comparative words when used as claims about this clinic or its doctors: best, top, leading, No.1/#1, most trusted, trusted (as a claim), world-class, premier, unmatched, renowned, expert (as an honorific), state-of-the-art, cutting-edge, "most advanced". Neutral technical uses stay, such as "advanced maternal age", "advanced endometriosis" or "best time to conceive".
2. Clinic-specific success rates, patient or baby counts, and "happy families" numbers are removed. General, population-level statistics in educational content are kept only when framed as general and approximate. They are listed for clinical verification because citations are absent.
3. "Guaranteed", "100%", "painless", "miracle", "cure" (as a promise) and "risk-free" are rewritten into balanced, individual-variation wording.
4. Testimonials, star ratings and review counts are no longer rendered or emitted in schema. Source data is left in git history only.
5. **URLs, slugs, routes, tracking, forms and layout are unchanged.** Slugs that contain "best" are flagged for a business and SEO decision (a 301 redirect plan) rather than renamed.
6. Nothing is invented: no registration numbers, citations, statistics or credentials.
